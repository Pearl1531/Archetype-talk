# Idea:2 Seamless background loading (Pre-fetching)

- **When:** When I'm heavily switching tracks one after another somewhere with weaker signal,

- **I want:** the app to automatically pre-load the start of the next 3 songs in the background,

- **So that:** the interface doesn't stutter and music changes instantly on tap.
