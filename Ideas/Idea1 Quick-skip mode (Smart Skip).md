# Idea:1 Quick-skip mode (Smart Skip)

- **When:** When I'm quickly clicking through a new, unfamiliar mix looking for workout energy,

- **I want:** to preview just the best 5 seconds of each song's chorus with one gesture (e.g. a swipe),

- **So that:** I quickly find a track I like, without frustrating waits for the whole song to load.
