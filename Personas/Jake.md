## Product usage

- **Plan:** Premium Student — right at the edge of affordability, consciously watches the price
- **Devices:** phone only; wireless earbuds, often on the move (campus, bus, gym)
- **When he listens:** daily in breaks and on the move; heavily in the evenings while studying
- **How he listens:** his own "mood" playlists + friends' playlists (from social links); lots of skipping between tracks
- **Features he uses:** creating and sharing playlists, saving friends' playlists, search (often unsuccessfully)
- **Features he avoids / doesn't use:** Discover Weekly (prefers people over the algorithm), podcasts (occasionally), manually building a taste profile ("too much work")
- **Social context:** playlists are a calling card — discovering and sharing music is a social activity for him
- **Competitor exposure:** half his friends on student [YouTube Premium](../Competitors/YouTube%20Music.md) (bundled with YouTube); openly tempted, held only by his playlists — [Tempted by YouTube Premium](../Signals/Tempted%20by%20YouTube%20Premium.md)

## Jobs to be Done

- When a friend shares a playlist, I want to save and explore it in one tap, so I discover new music without any effort. [Playlists are my identity](../Signals/Playlists%20are%20my%20identity.md)
- When I only remember a lyric or a melody, I want search to still find the track, so I don't give up and go back to an old playlist. [Can't find a song](../Signals/Can%27t%20find%20a%20song.md)
- When I build a playlist for a vibe, I want to share it in a way that looks good, so it becomes part of how friends see me.

## Pains

- [Can't find a song](../Signals/Can%27t%20find%20a%20song.md) Search fails on a lyric fragment or a melody — he gives up and returns to a known playlist
  - *In his words:* "I remember a snippet of lyrics, type it in, and… nothing." No theory about why — he just gives up "after a bit" and goes back to a playlist
- [Will downgrade to Free if price rises](../Signals/Will%20downgrade%20to%20Free%20if%20price%20rises.md) Price at the edge of worth-it — on a hike he rationally drops to Free
  - *In his words:* "I still count every dollar… music isn't something I'll spend a lot on" — matter-of-fact, not a complaint
- [Playlists are my identity](../Signals/Playlists%20are%20my%20identity.md) Playlist-sharing tools are poor relative to how social the ritual is
  - *In his words:* the sharing lives on stories, not in the app — "someone posts their playlist on a story, and I save it. That works better than anything the app recommends"
