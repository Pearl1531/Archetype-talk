# INT-02 — Jake

**M:** How do you discover new music?

**Jake (P):** Mostly from friends' playlists. Someone posts their playlist on a story, and I save it. That works better than anything the app recommends to me.

<!-- anchor: social -->
**M:** Do you share playlists yourself too?

**P:** Oh, for sure, I've got a few "for the mood." "Study," "Bar night," "Sad 3am hours." People ask me for them. It's kind of my calling card.

<!-- anchor: search -->
**M:** Does it ever happen that you can't find something?

**P:** All the time. I remember a snippet of lyrics, type it in, and… nothing. Or I know the melody but not the title. Then I give up after a bit and go back to a playlist.

<!-- anchor: budget -->
**M:** Do you pay for Premium?

**P:** I've got the student plan, but I still count every dollar. If they raised the price, honestly, I'd go back to the free version with ads. Music isn't something I'll spend a lot on.

<!-- anchor: discovery -->
**P:** I'd like discovery to just work on its own, without clicking around. But I don't feel like building a profile manually. If something takes effort, I won't do it.

<!-- anchor: youtube-bundle -->
**M:** What do your friends use?

**P:** Half my friend group has YouTube Premium on the student plan. They get YouTube without ads and music bundled in, one price. It's tempting, not gonna lie — because I'm on YouTube all the time anyway. The only thing keeping me here is that all my playlists are on Spotify.
