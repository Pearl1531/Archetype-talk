# INT-01 — Emma

**Moderator (M):** Tell me what a typical day with Spotify looks like for you.

**Emma (P):** Well… I turn on one playlist in the morning and that's it. Music plays in the background while I work. I don't listen to it actively — it's just there.

<!-- anchor: biblioteka -->
**M:** How do you manage your library?

**P:** Ha. Well, I don't, really. ==I've got something like two thousand liked songs and I can never find anything.=={pain, library-chaos} I end up playing the same five playlists on repeat. Actually organizing it would take forever.

<!-- anchor: shared-account -->
**M:** Is it your own account?

**P:** I share it with my boyfriend. He listens to metal, I listen to pop. Spotify has no idea who it's recommending to anymore. If he plays two hours of something heavy, I end up with it in my mixes afterward.

<!-- anchor: discover-weekly -->
**M:** What about Discover Weekly?

**P:** It used to be my favorite moment of the week. Now it's hit or miss. I've stopped counting on it. Probably because of parties — music plays from the speaker and Spotify thinks it's me listening.

<!-- anchor: mood -->
**P:** Sometimes it bugs me that it suggests sad tracks in the morning when I'm going for a run. ==I'd just like a button: I'm focused, I'm working out, or I'm feeling down. That's it.=={context-modes, feature-request}

<!-- anchor: premium-value -->
**M:** What do you think about the price of Premium?

**P:** I pay full price and I'm using a playlist from two years ago. I don't listen to podcasts. I kind of feel like I'm overpaying.

<!-- anchor: konkurencja -->
**M:** Have you ever considered another service?

**P:** My sister has Apple Music and says the sound quality is better for the same price. It's tempting sometimes. But ==I've got these playlists built up over years… moving all that over feels like a wall to me. I'm staying out of laziness, not love.=={switching-barriers}
