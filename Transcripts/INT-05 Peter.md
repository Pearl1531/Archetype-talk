# INT-05 — Peter

**M:** When do you listen most often?

**Peter (P):** In the car, on the way to work and back. Music in the morning, sometimes a podcast after work if I'm tired.

<!-- anchor: podcast-split -->
**M:** Do you use podcasts on Spotify?

**P:** Rarely. I pay for Premium mainly for the music and no ads. The whole podcasts-and-audiobooks side doesn't interest me — I feel like I'm paying extra for something I never touch.

<!-- anchor: focus -->
**M:** What about in the evening?

**P:** In the evening I want to wind down. I'll put on something calm, but the app keeps throwing in some high-energy track because I listened to something different in the morning. A "focus" or "evening" mode would help separate those.

<!-- anchor: price -->
**P:** If there were a cheaper plan with just music, I'd take it right away. I don't need the rest.
