# INT-06 — Tom

**M:** How do you use Spotify when you're on the road?

**Tom (P):** Almost exclusively by voice. In the car I can't really type anyway — hands on the wheel, eyes on the road. But honestly, even when I'm not driving, I'd rather dictate than type.

<!-- anchor: dyslexia -->
**M:** Why do you prefer speaking specifically?

**P:** I have dyslexia. Writing has always been slow and error-prone for me — I have to focus on every single word. Speaking is just faster, and I don't have to second-guess whether I spelled something right. It's not something that started in the car — it's just that in the car it makes even more sense, because typing would be dumb and dangerous anyway.

<!-- anchor: voice-misfire -->
**M:** Does voice search work well?

**P:** Depends, honestly. Sometimes I say a title and the app just doesn't get it at all — it plays something completely different or asks me to repeat it. With an artist's name that's spelled or pronounced in an unusual way, it's a total coin flip. I think my accent throws it off too.

**M:** What do you do then?

**P:** I repeat it once, twice, and if it still doesn't get it, I give up and put on something I already have saved. Sometimes, if I'm at a red light, I'll even try tapping through it on the screen, which is dumb anyway, because I waste time and still end up fumbling over what to tap.

<!-- anchor: workaround -->
**P:** It'd be great if it just worked the first time. I don't want to fight with the app, I want to hear the song.
