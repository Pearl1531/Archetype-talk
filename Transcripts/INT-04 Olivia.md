# INT-04 — Olivia

**M:** Who uses your account?

**Olivia (P):** Theoretically me, but the kids are constantly asking for cartoons and kids' songs. In the evening it's "Frozen" for the thousandth time.

<!-- anchor: kids -->
**M:** And what happens because of that?

**P:** My recommendations are now half kids' music. I sit down in the evening wanting something for myself, and there's "Baby Shark." The algorithm thinks that's me listening.

<!-- anchor: profiles -->
**M:** Do you have a family plan?

**P:** We have Family, but everyone still logs into the same account because it's more convenient for the kids on the tablet. I'd just like a separate profile for the kids so it doesn't mess up my recommendations. Like Netflix — you click who's watching.

<!-- anchor: reset -->
**P:** I dream of a "that wasn't me" button. To tell the app: ignore the last two weeks, that was the kids' stuff.
