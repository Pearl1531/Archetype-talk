# TEST-03 — Usability test: Smart Shuffle

Task: "Put on music to work to and listen as you normally would for 10 minutes."

<!-- anchor: skip -->
**P3 (thinking aloud):** Okay, starting up a mix… *(skips)* nope… *(skips)* not this either… *(skips)* God, why is it throwing this stuff at me.

*Observation: 4 skips within the first 10 seconds of random tracks.*

<!-- anchor: smart-shuffle -->
**P3:** This is that Smart Shuffle thing, right? It's mixing in songs I don't know among mine. I'd rather it just played what I picked. It's more annoying than helpful.

**M:** What would you normally do at this point?

**P3:** Honestly? I'd close the app and put something on YouTube instead. If it wears me out in the first minute, I don't have the patience.

<!-- anchor: prefetch -->
**P3:** And another thing — when I'm on the train and skipping around, it stutters. It takes a second to load before it starts playing. Annoying when I'm looking for hype music for a workout.

---

## Observer notes

- Skip-rage in the first few seconds; real risk of session abandonment.
- Smart Shuffle perceived as an intrusion into a deliberately chosen context.
- Noticeable loading delay when switching tracks quickly (weak signal/connectivity).
