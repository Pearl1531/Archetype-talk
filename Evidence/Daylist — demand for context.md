# Daylist — demand for context

## Content

Spotify launched **daylist** (September 2023) — a playlist that changes through the day with the listening context. It went viral: in January 2024 searches for "daylist" jumped **20,000%** after an Instagram trend, and Spotify expanded it globally to more languages (2024).

## Takeaways

- Demand for contextual listening (time of day, mood, activity) is huge and market-proven.
- Daylist's success shows users want the product to explicitly recognise their context — the ground for "listening modes".
