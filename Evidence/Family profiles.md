# Family profiles

## Content

On Spotify Community there have been **Live Ideas requests for multiple profiles under one account** for years (the Netflix/Disney+ pattern). The motivation in threads: separate libraries, Daily Mixes and Wrapped without setting up separate accounts with separate emails; quick profile switching on shared devices (tablet, speaker), so one person's listening doesn't break another's recommendations.

## Takeaways

- The need for profile separation is stated plainly by the community — for years, repeatedly, across threads.
- The Family plan solves payment, but not sharing a single profile on shared devices.
