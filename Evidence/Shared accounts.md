# Shared accounts

## Content

Per data cited by Music Business Worldwide, **22.2% of Spotify subscribers** share or receive account access via a shared password. The industry has debated for years whether Spotify should follow Netflix and crack down — for now many accounts genuinely serve more than one person.

## Takeaways

- A significant share of accounts have a "mixed" taste profile — the recommendation model doesn't know who it serves.
- Until there's profile separation, sharing structurally breaks personalisation.
