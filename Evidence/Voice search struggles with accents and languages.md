# Voice search struggles with accents and languages

## Content

Spotify Community threads document recurring voice-search failures tied to accented or non-English pronunciation, particularly for foreign-language song and artist names — users report the feature misunderstanding requests specifically "because of your accent when pronouncing foreign language song names." Separately, Spotify removed in-app voice search ("Hey Spotify") when its Car Thing hardware launched, then discontinued Car Thing itself, leaving a active community request thread asking for voice search to be reinstated in the app and on Android Auto.

## Takeaways

- Voice search failure isn't a hypothetical edge case — it's an openly, repeatedly reported problem specifically for non-English names and accented speech.
- Voice input in the car is not just a "nice to have" for Spotify's own user base — its removal (via Car Thing, then "Hey Spotify") generated active pushback and reinstatement requests.
