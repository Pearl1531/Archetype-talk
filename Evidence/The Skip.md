# The Skip

## Content

Analysis of billions of plays on Spotify (Paul Lamere, The Echo Nest/Spotify): **24.14% of tracks are skipped in the first 5 seconds**, and **48.6% of all plays** end in a skip before the song finishes. The vast majority of skips happen in the first ~20 seconds; the average listener skips a track roughly every ~4 minutes.

## Takeaways

- The first seconds of a song decide it — listeners judge a recommendation almost instantly.
- A high skip rate at the start of a session is a strong signal of recommendation mismatch.
