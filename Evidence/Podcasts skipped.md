# Podcasts skipped

## Content

Only about **32% of Spotify users** engage with podcasts (~193M of ~602M MAU, 2024). That means **~68% of users don't engage with spoken content** — even though the platform invests heavily in podcasts and audiobooks and features them on the home screen.

## Takeaways

- For most users Spotify remains a music app — spoken content is an invisible (yet co-paid-for) part of the offering.
- Podcast exposure on the home screen hits a minority, irritating the "music only" segment.
