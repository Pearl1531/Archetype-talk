# Price sensitivity

## Content

In a consumer study **43% of respondents** said they'd likely cancel an online subscription they consider "too costly"; other reasons include consolidating services (20%) and the end of a promo (18%). Statista confirms price is the main reason for cancelling streaming services.

## Takeaways

- Price is the top reason for churn — users regularly audit whether the subscription "adds up".
- Users paying for features they don't use are the first to leave on a price hike.
