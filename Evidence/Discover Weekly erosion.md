# Discover Weekly erosion

## Content

On Spotify Community and forums users have reported **Discover Weekly degradation** for years: "constantly terrible", "getting worse month by month". The recurring mechanism is **context pollution** — e.g. a ResetEra user describes how sleep sounds completely took over their Discover Weekly, crowding out new music. Others point to covers instead of originals and the lost "magic" of the feature's early years.

## Takeaways

- DW degradation is a widely documented community problem, not a single anecdote.
- The model learns from every session without distinguishing context — one unusual stretch of listening can "hijack" recommendations.
