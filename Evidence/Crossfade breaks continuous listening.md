# Crossfade breaks continuous listening

## Content

Spotify's crossfade blends the end of one track into the start of the next. For albums and mixes designed as one continuous piece (DJ mixes, concept albums, tracks that flow into each other by design), crossfade overlaps intentional silences, applause, or transitions the artist placed on purpose — audibly breaking the intended listening experience. Community threads document users repeatedly unable to fully turn crossfade off (it re-enables itself, especially on certain curated playlists or after updates), and asking Spotify for a persistent, reliable off switch.

## Takeaways

- The problem isn't crossfade existing — it's that it overrides artist-authored transitions on continuous works, and the "off" setting doesn't reliably stick.
- This is a recurring, multi-year community complaint, not a one-off preference.
