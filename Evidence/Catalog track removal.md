# Catalog track removal

## Content

Tracks and whole catalogs periodically disappear from Spotify without warning when licensing deals between labels/distributors and Spotify expire or fall through — e.g. Spotify pulling hundreds of K-Pop tracks globally after an expired Kakao M licensing deal, or users reporting an artist's near-complete catalog ("The Fixx") vanishing overnight. Spotify's own terms state it may remove content "without notice." Community threads consistently report finding out only by noticing a playlist got shorter — no in-app notification, no email.

## Takeaways

- Catalog removal is a structural licensing risk, not a bug — it recurs across labels/regions and is documented over years of community threads.
- The absence of any removal notification (in-app or email) is the recurring complaint, not the removal itself — users accept licensing exists, but want to know when it hits their library.
