# Students and social

## Content

18–24 users are **~26% of Spotify's base** (the second-largest segment after 25–34 = ~29%). Playlist sharing is rising — industry reports note **playlist shares up ~47% YoY**, driven partly by TikTok integrations and social trends (like the viral daylist).

## Takeaways

- For the young segment playlists are social and identity-forming — a channel for discovery and growth.
- Making sharing easier (nice cards, social integrations) is an acquisition lever in the student segment.
