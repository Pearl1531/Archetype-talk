# Smart Shuffle complaints

## Content

On the official Spotify Community there are many threads and Live Ideas where Premium users demand the ability to **permanently disable Smart Shuffle**. The titles speak for themselves: "Smart Shuffle Will NOT Turn off. Worst feature ever made". Community pressure led to adding a setting to turn it off (mobile first, then desktop).

## Takeaways

- Injecting unknown tracks into a deliberately chosen playlist is perceived as an intrusion, not help.
- The volume of votes forced Spotify to change — this is a confirmed, not hypothetical, problem.
