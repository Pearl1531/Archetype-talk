# Library problems

## Content

Users report on Spotify Community that they **can't find tracks in their own library**: search doesn't work inside all playlists, and Liked Songs lacks sorting and filters. Users describe **re-adding the same albums and tracks**, because they can't find them at home.

## Takeaways

- Saving works, but retrieving saved content doesn't; the library grows faster than the tools to navigate it.
- "I have everything, so I have nothing" is a real, documented pattern, not an anecdote.
