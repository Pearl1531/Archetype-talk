# Skipping in academic research

## Content

Academic research on streaming data confirms that skipping is a **universal, systematic behaviour**, linked to a song's musical structure (e.g. the moment the vocal enters) and the session context. Skip patterns are regular enough to serve as a signal of recommendation quality.

## Takeaways

- A skip isn't noise — it's a measurable, repeatable signal that content doesn't fit the listener and context.
- The product can use early skips as real-time feedback.
