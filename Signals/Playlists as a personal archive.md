# Playlists as a personal archive

Zoe runs a deliberate curation system: playlists split by genre and purpose ("ambient for work", "techno for running"), a working "new stuff to check out" list she empties every Sunday, and chronological archives — "Zoe 2022", "Zoe 2023" — she describes as personal yearbooks. She names the effort herself and rejects the framing that it is work.

> "It is, but it's enjoyable for me, it's not a chore."
