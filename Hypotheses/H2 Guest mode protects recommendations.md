# Hypothesis: Guest mode protects recommendations

- **If:** we add a one-tap "guest is listening" mode,

- **By:** excluding everything played in that mode from taste modeling,

- **Will:** listeners who share speakers or accounts regain trust in Discover Weekly within a few weeks,

- **Because:** we assume the distrust comes from a few polluting sessions (parties, partners, kids), not from the recommender itself.
