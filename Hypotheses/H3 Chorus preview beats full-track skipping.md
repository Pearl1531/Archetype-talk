# Hypothesis: Chorus preview beats full-track skipping

- **If:** we let listeners sample just the chorus of unfamiliar tracks,

- **By:** a one-gesture 5-second preview,

- **Will:** exploratory sessions end with a pick instead of skip fatigue,

- **Because:** we assumed skipping is a search behavior, not a rejection of the catalog.
