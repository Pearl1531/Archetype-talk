# The Value Auditor

- **Short description:** Constantly weighs whether the price matches the features actually used. Pays for music and no ads, not for podcasts or audiobooks. Sensitive to price hikes; when it gets too expensive they rationally drop to a free plan or find a workaround.

- **Link to persona:** [Jake](../Personas/Jake.md) (student plan) threatens to drop to Free — [Will downgrade to Free if price rises](../Signals/Will%20downgrade%20to%20Free%20if%20price%20rises.md). The pattern also shows in "music-only" payers — [Pays for podcasts never listened to](../Signals/Pays%20for%20podcasts%20never%20listened%20to.md).

- **Core pain:** The feeling of paying extra for features they don't use.

- **Resists agreeing about:** "Price isn't really a big factor for you, is it?" — no; they audit value constantly and will say plainly they'd downgrade or drop to Free rather than pretend price doesn't matter.
