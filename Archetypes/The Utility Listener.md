# The Utility Listener

- **Short description:** Treats music as a tool (a background utility) that eases daily life, not as a hobby. Music should help with focus, a workout, or relaxing. They don't want to waste time searching — the app should just work, effortlessly.

- **Link to persona:** [Emma](../Personas/Emma.md) treats Spotify as a habit, not a passion — opens the app, plays the same playlist, and gets back to work. She wants explicit modes instead of searching — [Wants a focus mode](../Signals/Wants%20a%20focus%20mode.md).

- **Core pain:** Frustration when the algorithm forces active clicking and searching instead of playing the right background.

## Questions by context

- **[Playlist editing]** How do I loop just this one song, not the whole playlist? `assumption`
- **[Multitasking]** I saw a friend watch a video while music kept playing in the background — is that possible here? `assumption`
- **[Playlist editing]** Can I just let it keep playing similar songs after my playlist ends, without picking a new one myself? `assumption`
- **[Focus / work]** Is there a way to say "play this kind of thing" without typing anything? [Wants a focus mode](../Signals/Wants%20a%20focus%20mode.md)
