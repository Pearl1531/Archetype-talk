# The Passive Discoverer

- **Short description:** Wants discovery to “just happen” but won't do the curation work. Relies on recommendations and other people's playlists; abandons the process the moment it requires clicking and building a profile by hand.

- **Link to persona:** [Jake](../Personas/Jake.md) discovers music from friends' playlists and won't build a profile manually — [Playlists are my identity](../Signals/Playlists%20are%20my%20identity.md). [Emma](../Personas/Emma.md) abandoned Discover Weekly once it started failing — [Doesn't trust Discover Weekly](../Signals/Doesn%27t%20trust%20Discover%20Weekly.md).

- **Core pain:** Discovery demands effort they won't put in — and the automation they rely on fails after the profile is “polluted”.
