# The Social Curator

- **Short description:** Treats playlists as identity and a social language. Builds them “by mood”, shares them, and discovers music mainly through friends. For them the library isn't storage — it's a way to show who they are.

- **Link to persona:** [Jake](../Personas/Jake.md) has "mood" playlists that are his calling card and that friends ask him for — [Playlists are my identity](../Signals/Playlists%20are%20my%20identity.md).

- **Core pain:** Sharing and personalisation tools are poor relative to how social a ritual listening is for them.
