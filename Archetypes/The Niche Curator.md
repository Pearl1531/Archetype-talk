# The Niche Curator

- **Short description:** Runs a deliberate system over their own collection — playlists by purpose, a weekly triage ritual, chronological archives — and treats streaming as the browsing layer before real ownership. The deeper into a niche they go, the less the product helps: recommendations pull back toward the mainstream, and the catalogue itself can't be relied on to still hold what they filed. Curation is the pleasure, not the chore; being undone by the platform is the injury.

- **Link to persona:** [Zoe](../Personas/Zoe.md) keeps genre playlists, a Sunday triage list and yearly archives — [Playlists as a personal archive](../Signals/Playlists%20as%20a%20personal%20archive.md) — while a single mainstream play swamps her recommendations for a fortnight — [Drift toward mainstream](../Signals/Drift%20toward%20mainstream.md). Tracks vanish from her playlists with no notice — [Tracks disappear without warning](../Signals/Tracks%20disappear%20without%20warning.md) — and imposed crossfade breaks albums meant as one continuous piece — [Crossfade breaks mixes and albums](../Signals/Crossfade%20breaks%20mixes%20and%20albums.md).

- **Core pain:** The product is built for the mainstream listener, and every deliberate act of curation is fighting it — the further into a niche, the less help there is, exactly where help would be worth most.
