# Research backlog — questions from persona sessions

A collector of research questions that emerged from AI persona conversations (`/persona-talk`) and queries (`/persona-query`).

> ## ⚠️ Overriding rule
> **A synthetic persona conversation NEVER creates Signals or Evidence.**
> A persona is a synthesis of existing data — not a new source. A conversation can only
> **generate questions for real research** (interviews, tests, surveys with people).
> A `Signal` is created only after contact with a live user; then → `/extract-findings`.

## Where this comes from

- The "Researcher context" block in `/persona-talk` ends with a **"Questions to dig into"** section.
- When you hit a persona's **"Known unknown"** (an L1 gap — assumption-level, no data) — that lands here too.
- Occasionally, `/persona-talk` names a **"Blind spot"** — a question neither the researcher nor the persona's data raised at all during a session, not a gap the data already flagged. Rare by design — most sessions won't produce one.
- On `[exit]` the skill offers to append these questions to this list.

## The columns

`Kind` — `qualitative` / `quantitative` / `mixed`: the **shape of the answer** that would
settle the question, which decides the method (and a method decides where the answer
lands: a session we run makes a `Signal`, an aggregate makes `Evidence`).

`Priority` — `critical` / `major` / `minor`: how much rests on the answer. An AI run
writes the bare word and may revise it later. **A value ending in `(locked)` was set by
the researcher by hand — no AI run may change, clear or re-word it**, only disagree with
it out loud. An empty cell means nobody has decided; the app shows a guess read from the
wording and writes nothing.
